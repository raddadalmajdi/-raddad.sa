About Eigo Media / 英語メディアについて

英語

Eigo (英語) means "English" in Japanese

Eigo Media (英語メディア) is an English language learning platform built specifically for Japanese speakers. The name combines Eigo (英語 = English) with Media to create an accessible English learning experience for Japan.

Founded in 1997, eigomedia.com has been a trusted English education resource in Japan for nearly three decades. With 647 referring Japanese websites, the platform has become one of the established destinations for Japanese learners seeking to master English.
