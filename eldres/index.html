About Eldres

Eldres is a senior care management platform designed for elderly care facilities in the greater Boston area, with a primary focus on West Roxbury, Massachusetts. Founded in 2001, eldres.com has been a trusted resource in the elder care community for over two decades.

The platform connects care facilities, families, and healthcare coordinators through a unified digital experience — making daily care management more transparent and efficient.
